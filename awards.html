The awards

Theme Sankalp Se Prabhao Tak

A national platform recognising ideas, initiatives, leaders, and organisations creating meaningful change across India.

Our vision: to create a credible national platform that discovers, recognises, and celebrates meaningful sustainability impact across India. We recognise innovation, environmental impact, leadership, and transformation.

Event agenda

  • Summit conversations and thought leadership
  • Innovation and impact showcases
  • Expert jury recognition and award honours
  • Networking, partnerships, and national visibility

Core focus areas

  • Clean energy
  • Climate technology
  • EV and mobility
  • Circular economy
  • Green infrastructure
  • Sustainable business
  • Inclusive action

The Awards are the evening component of the NBT Vasudha Summit — 13 February 2027, Bharat Mandapam. Delegate passes are on sale.

Judging

How an entry is judged

Five criteria, applied to every entry.

Innovation

Originality, technological depth, and breakthrough approach.

Environmental & Social Impact

Quantifiable contribution and real-world outcomes.

Measurable Results

Key performance indicators, verified data, case studies, and supporting evidence.

Scalability & Vision

Potential to expand across sectors, regions, and markets.

Leadership & Value

Execution excellence, governance, resilience, and long-term value.

The jury

Independent, and blind to payment

An independent panel drawn from:

  • Environmental science & policy
  • Rural development practice
  • Corporate sustainability leadership
  • Public finance
None of whom report to Navbharat Times or Times Internet.
None of whom see which entries have paid an entry fee until after scoring is complete.

Awards — questions

Questions

Do I need to attend the ceremony to win?

No, but winners are announced and felicitated in person at Bharat Mandapam on 13 February 2027, and recipients are strongly encouraged to attend.

Is the entry fee refundable?

No. The nomination fee covers the cost of jury review and assessment regardless of outcome, and is non-refundable once submitted.

Does paying the entry fee affect my chances of winning?

No. The fee pays for the assessment process, not the outcome — jurors score entries without visibility into payment status.
