FAQs

Questions

Do I need to attend the ceremony to win?

No, but winners are felicitated in person at the awards night and attendance is strongly encouraged.

Is the entry fee refundable?

No. The fee covers jury review and assessment regardless of outcome.

Does paying the fee affect my chances?

No. Jurors score entries without visibility into payment status.

Can I nominate in more than one category?

Yes. Higher plans include two or three category nominations.
