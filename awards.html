Questions, answered.

When and where are the NBT Vasudha Sustainability Awards 2026?

The awards night is on 13 February 2027 from 6:30 PM at Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, New Delhi, on the evening of NBT Vasudha Summit 2027. There are 34 categories across four tracks, and nomination plans start at ₹8,999 + GST.

Do I need to attend the ceremony to win?

Winners are announced at the awards night at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 13 February 2027. Our team will confirm attendance details with you after your nomination is accepted.

Is the entry fee refundable?

Refund terms are set out in our Cancellation Policy. Please read it before you pay.

Does paying the fee affect my chances?

No. The fee does not guarantee an award. An independent jury scores every entry on the same criteria.

Can I nominate in more than one category?

Yes. Individual Acceleration and Corporate Growth cover 2 categories, and Corporate Acceleration covers 3.
